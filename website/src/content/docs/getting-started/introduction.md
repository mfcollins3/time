Naked Time is a time planning and tracking application. Its goal is to make
you more productive in completing value-building activities by letting you
hyperfocus on what is important and tracking how much time you spend completing
activities.

Naked Time implements the
[Pomodoro Technique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomodoro_Technique), a
popular time management technique where work is broken down into 25-minute
intervals followed by a short break. The technique helps you avoid burnout by
time-boxing work into smaller intervals and incrementally completing assigned
tasks, and it measures and records the results of your work so that you can
review your performance and optimize your use of time.

## Who is Naked Time for?

* Students
* Individuals
* Solo software developers
* Independent contractors, freelancers, and gig workers who charge based on
  time worked
* Small software teams
* Businesses of all sizes that need to track their employees' time
